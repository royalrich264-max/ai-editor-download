# AI Editor for Windows

**Long video in. Ready-to-post clips out.**

AI Editor finds the best moments in your long videos, cuts them into short vertical clips with captions (in Kinyarwanda and 99 other languages), and posts them to your YouTube and TikTok accounts. Everything runs on your own PC.

## ⬇ Download

**[Download AI-Editor-Setup.exe](https://github.com/royalrich264-max/ai-editor-download/releases/latest/download/AI-Editor-Setup.exe)** (about 425 MB)

Or see every version on the [Releases page](https://github.com/royalrich264-max/ai-editor-download/releases).

**Needs:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). 8 GB of memory recommended.

## Install

1. Open **AI-Editor-Setup.exe** from your Downloads folder.
2. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
   This appears for new apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate yet.
3. Choose where to install it (no administrator rights needed) and click **Install**.
4. Open **AI Editor** from your desktop.

**Kinyarwanda captions:** after installing, open **Settings > Languages** and click **Install add-on** once (about 2.5 GB).
